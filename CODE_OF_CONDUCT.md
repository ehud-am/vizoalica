# Code of conduct

Vizoalica is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, experience, and reasons for
using it. Everyone is welcome here, and everyone is expected to help keep it that way. This code
applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space for the project. It is adapted
from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to self-hosting.
- Assume good intent, and say so when something is unclear rather than when it is wrong.
- Disagree with the idea, not the person. Give feedback that someone can act on.
- Give credit, and thank people for their time. Every contribution, including a good bug report,
  is work.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's personal information, and do not paste credentials,
  tokens, account identifiers, or visitor data (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about a person's identity, background, or abilities.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact after being asked to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressure on maintainers to act on a schedule.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying these standards and may edit, hide, or remove
comments, issues, and contributions, or restrict participation, when they do not follow them. They
will explain the reason where it is safe to do so.

To report a problem, message the maintainer, [@ehud-am](https://github.com/ehud-am), using the
contact details on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the item
itself. Reports are handled privately and in good faith, and the reporter's identity is not shared
without their agreement. A conduct report is not a security report: for vulnerabilities, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

If a report is about the maintainer, use GitHub's **Report content** option or contact GitHub
Support, which can act independently.
